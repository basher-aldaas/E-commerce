## Result of the worker run above (honest reading)
The worker consumed all 4 messages that survived the recreated broker (orders 10, 11, 12 and 13, the last one
published by the relay after the outage), so the queues and the processing survived. The emails themselves
failed: Mailtrap answered "535 5.7.0 Too many failed login attempts". Cause: scenario 4 injected the failure
with a wrong SMTP password, and the repeated bad logins locked the Mailtrap username for a while, so even the
correct password was refused. The worker behaved as designed (no ACK, 4 attempts each, then DLQ: 0 lost),
but no email was delivered in this run. Fix for the method: inject SMTP failures with an unreachable host
(MAIL_HOST=smtp.invalid), never with a wrong password. The 5 dead letters (orders 9-13) are replayed later
with `php artisan notifications:replay-dead-letters` once the lock expires (see 06-dlq-replay.txt).
